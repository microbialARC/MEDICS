### Genomic context of the locus

The entropy scan does not use annotation; it ranks windows on entropy alone.
Even so, the selected window is not arbitrary. Locus 13 lies within
`PANCAC_00500` (547,751–550,738, + strand), annotated as a **Ser-Asp rich
fibrinogen-binding, bone sialoprotein-binding protein**. This is an
*sdr*-family MSCRAMM surface adhesin. The candidate locus plot
(`results/figures/locus_candidates/locus_13.pdf`) shows 31 informative sites
across the 500-bp interior, with both 100-bp flanks entirely invariant.

## Resolution beyond MLST

Among typed isolates within the study period:

| Metric | Count |
| --- | --- |
| Sequence types (STs) present | **58** |
| STs with at least one ST-specific haplotype | **46** |

An *ST-specific haplotype* is a haplotype type observed in exactly one ST.
Counting ST-specific haplotypes within each ST shows how finely the locus
subdivides that ST, which is the resolution it adds beyond MLST.

Across these 46 STs (`results/tables/per_st_haplotype_counts.csv`):

| --- | --- |
| Range | 1–124 |
| Median (IQR) | **2.5** (1–8.75) |
| Mean (SD) | **9.83** (20.47) |
